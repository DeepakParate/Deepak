// Physical Infrastructure

Datacenter Infrastructure

Comprehensive enterprise datacenter operations covering power, cooling, physical security, and network fabric

Power Infrastructure

Dual-feed redundant power distribution with N+1 UPS configuration and automatic failover. Managed PDUs with per-outlet monitoring and remote power cycling capabilities across all racks.

N+1 UPS Dual PDU APC / Eaton Remote PDU 2N Power Feed

Cooling & HVAC

Precision air conditioning with hot-aisle/cold-aisle containment design. Computer Room Air Handlers (CRAH) maintain optimal operating temperature between 18–27°C with humidity control.

Hot/Cold Aisle CRAH Units 18–27°C Range N+1 Redundancy

Physical Security

Multi-layer physical access controls with biometric entry, mantrap systems, and 24/7 CCTV monitoring. Individual rack-level locking with electronic access logs and visitor escorting policies.

Biometric Access CCTV 24/7 Rack Locks Mantrap Access Logs

Network Fabric

Leaf-spine network topology with 10/25/100 GbE connectivity. Cisco Nexus core switches provide high-availability inter-rack communication with BGP routing and VXLAN fabric overlay.

Leaf-Spine Cisco Nexus 10/25/100GbE BGP / VXLAN

Fire Suppression

Pre-action dry pipe sprinkler system combined with VESDA (Very Early Smoke Detection Apparatus) and clean agent FM-200 suppression for server rooms, ensuring zero data loss during suppression events.

FM-200 VESDA Pre-Action Zone Control

Remote Hands & DCIM

Datacenter Infrastructure Management (DCIM) platform for real-time capacity planning, asset tracking, and environmental monitoring. Integrated with ITSM for change management and remote hands ticketing.

DCIM Asset Tracking iDRAC / iLO ServiceNow
